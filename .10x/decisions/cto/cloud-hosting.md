# CTO — cloud-hosting
- Buy vs build: self-host the existing app instead of rewriting for serverless. A managed PaaS is ruled out (MediaPipe needs too much RAM for free tiers, and there's no free disk).
- Spend: about $11/yr for the .com domain; everything else is on free tiers (Oracle Always Free, Cloudflare Zero Trust free, B2 first 10 GB).
- See ADR-003.
